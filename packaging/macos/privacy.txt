Heyo. you like privacy? we do too.

so that's why it's local and private. no account, no key, no
upload, no telemetry, no crash reports, no analytics. the server binds to
127.0.0.1, which means it cannot be reached from another machine on your
own wifi, never mind from anywhere else.

keeper writes one folder, .keeper, next to your photographs. thumbnails,
your tags, your crops. delete it and the archive is exactly as it was. no
original is ever edited. one key can move a file, it asks first every time,
and it goes through the trash so the machine can put it back.

there is exactly one thing in here that can reach the network, and it is
off. keeper can check github for a newer keeper, it asks you once before it
ever does, and saying no means it never asks again and never makes the
request. when you say yes it is one GET for one small file: no identifier,
no archive, no counts, no cookie.

you found this by turning on hidden files, which is a reasonable thing to
do to something you just downloaded. all of the above is checkable: it is
open source, apache 2.0, github.com/gntrs/keeper
